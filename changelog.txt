Version: 1.1.4
Date: 2026-09-18
  Features:
    - New "Longest trip" history row, showing the items that bots carry furthest from pickup to delivery. The tooltip shows the average and median trip too. Long trips were easy to miss before, because an item also delivered in bulk over short distances (e.g. to a mall) has a low average Ticks/item.
    - Click an item in the Longest trip row to show up to 5 of its longest trips on the map, at most one per destination. Each trip's ends are outlined and joined by a line with direction arrows, and the one shown is labelled with the item. Click again to move on to the next trip, or right-click to go to the pickup end of the one shown. Trips stay on the map three times as long as other highlights, 30 seconds by default. The tooltip lists the longest trips too.
    - Shift+click an item in the Longest trip row to ignore the trip shown on the map, when that long trip is expected. Trips of that item to that destination are then no longer listed, though they still count in the item's statistics. Ignored items and destinations are listed in the network settings, where they can be shown on the map or removed.
    - New suggestion when an item is regularly carried much further than usual, e.g. to an outpost when it mostly goes to a nearby mall: a trip of at least 200 m, at least 4 times the item's median trip, made at least 3 times, and still happening. Click to show the trip on the map, or Shift+click to exclude it if it's expected.
    - Two new map settings tune that suggestion, because what counts as a long way depends on the base. "Long trip suggestions" sets how much further than an item's typical trip it has to be carried before it is flagged: Relaxed 8 times, Normal 4, Sensitive twice, or Off to turn the suggestion off altogether. "Minimum distance for a long trip" sets how far the trip has to be in the first place. A trip also stops being flagged once it has not happened for as long as "Keep old suggestions", or for three minutes if that is shorter, so the suggestion goes away by itself once the route is fixed. The suggestion is made from the delivery history, so it only appears for networks watched with the History rows shown.
    - New per-player setting "Show estimated trip starts" to leave the dashed estimate off the map and show only what was actually observed.
    - When a bot's pickup is not observed, the trip is estimated from where the bot was first seen delivering. The tooltip shows how many trips were measured and how many estimated. On the map such a trip is extended backwards by a dashed line, showing how much further back the pickup could have been. Bots fly straight, so the direction is known. If the bot had been seen before, it can only have flown one scan pass unnoticed, so the line is short; if it was first seen already carrying, it could have come from anywhere in the network, and the line reaches as far back as the network does. The start of such a trip is ringed rather than outlined like a chest, because nothing stands there.
  Changes:
    - Clicking an item in the Longest trip row, or the long trip suggestion, no longer repeats itself. A click that would redraw what is already on the map moves on to the next trip instead, and the other mouse button shows the other end of the trip being shown, so a delivery can still be paired with where it came from. The row's tooltip says which of the two the next click will do.
    - Distances are shown in metres and kilometres rather than tiles, which are the same thing: one tile is one metre. Long trips and totals read more easily, e.g. 1.3 km instead of 1251 tiles.
    - The Ticks/item row is replaced by Distance carried: the total distance bots have carried each item. Ticks/item mixed up distance, time spent waiting to charge, and how many items each trip carried, and a few long trips were easily hidden by many short ones. Distance carried shows which items take up most of your bots' work, and Longest trip finds the outliers. Charging delays are still shown in the Activity row and suggestions.
    - Item tooltips in Distance carried and Longest trip show the median trip next to the average. An average much higher than the median means a few long trips among many short ones.
    - When a long trip has been ignored, the Longest trip tooltip now says how long the longest trip is with the ignored ones counted, so it is clear what the ignore list is hiding.
    - Ignoring an item for undersupply, or ignoring the filter mismatch for a suggestion's chests, now confirms itself where you clicked, the way ignoring a long trip already did. The message says what was ignored and points at the network settings, where the list can be shown on the map or the entry removed again.
    - Clearing the delivery history no longer closes the network settings pane, and no longer rebuilds the whole window to empty three rows.
  Bugfixes:
    - Fixed a multiplayer desync when a player joined a game. The scheduler spread its heavier tasks across ticks from whatever tick each computer had started at, so a player who joined later ran a bot count on a different tick from the host. Task timing now depends only on the game tick, and is rebuilt on every computer at once when a player joins or leaves.
    - A game frozen from the Logistics Insights window resumed by itself whenever any player's window was rebuilt: a change to a per-player setting, or a player joining. Rebuilding a window now leaves the freeze alone.
    - Saving the game in the few ticks between a scan starting and its first chunk, roughly one autosave in ten while the window was open, made the loaded game finish that scan with nothing in it. Every delivery being followed was recorded as finished, the Activity row showed no bots delivering, and a red "no storage" suggestion appeared until the next pass. Such a scan is now abandoned and started again.
    - The per-network setting "Ignore when no storage" silenced the low storage suggestions altogether, full chests included. It now only silences them for a network that has no storage chests of that kind, as described.
    - The "Chunk interval" setting had no effect: bots were counted every 5 ticks whatever it was set to. It now sets how often a chunk of bots is counted, as described, and its default is now 7 ticks, close to the cadence saves have actually been running at. Lower it to track deliveries more closely, at more CPU.
    - The "UI update interval" setting was forgotten every time a save was loaded, and only took effect again once it was changed. It also left a player who joined a multiplayer game after the setting was changed on a different schedule from everyone else. It is now re-applied on load.
    - A background refresh could start on the network you had just walked into, while its foreground scan was still running. Both scans share one counter, so the pass was restarted without history gathering and finished by dropping every delivery it had been tracking, unrecorded. Background refresh now leaves the foreground network alone, and a foreground scan starting on a network mid-background-refresh takes it over instead of running alongside it.
    - Suggestions and undersupply were recalculated continuously for any network a player was standing in, even with the Logistics Insights window closed, so the analysis never rested while anyone was inside a network. A network now only gets the fast foreground analysis while somebody has the window open on it; otherwise it is analysed at the background refresh interval, like it is scanned.
    - Changing "Items to process at a time" cut every running scan short and published the half-finished pass as a complete one, so each bot not yet reached had its delivery recorded as finished. The new chunk size now simply applies from the next pass.
    - Turning "Calculate undersupply" on or off only rebuilt the window of the player who changed it, leaving other players with a frozen or missing Undersupply row, and errored when changed from the server console. Turning it off also left the last calculated shortages counted in the mini window tooltip. Every player's window is now rebuilt, and the old results are dropped.
    - With "Show all networks" off, a network you had just left stayed in the Networks window, and in the mini window's counts, until its history expired a few minutes later. It is now listed only while somebody is in it, though its history is still kept for the grace period in case you come back.
    - Delivery history is no longer lost when you look at a long trip on the map. Showing a trip moves you into remote view at its destination, which counts as leaving the network, and the history was thrown away the moment you did. A network's history is now kept for two minutes after the last player leaves it, so stepping out of coverage or taking a look around and coming back no longer loses it.
    - "History data: Active for" now stops while nobody is watching a network, and no longer resets when another player walks into a network you are already watching.
    - Turning off "Show history" stopped the bot scan from starting at all, because it tested a gathering flag under a name nothing ever set. The Activity row's Delivering and Picking counts were cleared, and the other bot counts only updated as often as the background refresh, or not at all when background refresh was turned off. Hiding the history row now only turns off history gathering, as intended.
    - Networks nobody was watching built up a delivery history of their own. A background scan only looks at a network every few seconds, so a bot is usually delivering somewhere new by the next look, and finishing the previous delivery recorded it whether or not any player had asked for history. Those records had no trip distance, so the Totals row disagreed with Distance carried, and once a network's unwatched history had expired it was never cleared again.
    - A history or delivery row could keep showing the network you just left, when the two networks happened to have had the same number of updates.
    - Shift+click in the Longest trip row could stop listing a trip you had never looked at. The trip on the map was remembered without which network it belonged to, so after moving into another network that carries the same item, the row still offered to ignore "the trip shown" and excluded that network's trip instead. The same staleness made the first click in the new network carry on from where the last one left off rather than starting at the longest trip.
    - The Longest trip tooltip could offer to stop listing a trip that the click would not act on, in multiplayer, once another player had ignored a trip of the same item. The tooltip and the click now agree on which trip is on the map.
    - A trip could claim to be measured from a pickup chest the bot left minutes earlier. Where a bot is picking up is only forgotten during a scan, and a network nobody is watching need not be scanned at all, so a stale pickup survived until the player came back and was then matched to whatever the bot was carrying by then. Returning to a network now forgets them, as it already forgot which bots it had been watching.
    - A pickup of one quality could be credited as the start of a delivery of another quality of the same item, giving that trip a measured distance from the wrong chest.
    - The reset buttons in the network settings could show a missing-text tooltip.

Version: 1.1.0
Date: 2026-04-10
  Features:
    - Logistics Insights now is easier on the CPU, with optimisations and new settings to better control the performance/accuracy/timeliness tradeoff.
    - New setting to reduce the impact of undersupply and suggestion calculations, by reducing the chunk size by a factor compared to the (much cheaper) bot activity scanning. The default is 4, which reduces the overhead and delays the appearance of data.
    - New setting to scan only every Nth requester for undersupply calculations each sweep, which further reduces the overhead and delays the appearance of undersupply data. The default is 3, trading off some timeliness for performance.
  Changes:
    - Performance improvements in scanning bot and roboport networks as well as UI updates, reducing raw CPU usage by 3-35%.
    - Reduced UI update overhead through caching and skip-when-unchanged logic, for smoother performance with windows open.
    - Improvements to the task scheduler, which spreads analysis overhead across more ticks, reducing the size of spikes in CPU usage.
    - Increased the default background refresh interval from 10 to 30 seconds
    - Stopped calculating # of providers for background networks, and reduced the frequency for foreground networks. This turned out to be disproportionately expensive!
    - Background scans are now distributed evenly across the refresh interval, instead of running back-to-back in bursts
    - Added benchmarking framework for dev use that can be used to find bottlenecks and quantify improvements.
  Bugfixes:
    - Fixed edge cases in migration from earlier versions.
    - Fix that progress indicators could briefly show "-100%", which obviously doesn't make sense.
